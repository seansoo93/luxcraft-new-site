HDB @ 782 Pasir Ris St 71

A masterful tribute to mid-century modern character, warm terracotta tiling, bespoke walnut carpentry, and geometric breeze blocks curated for relaxed coastal living in Pasir Ris.

Mid-Century Modern Warmth, Breeze Block Character & Bespoke Craftsmanship

Nestled in the coastal enclave of Pasir Ris, this resale HDB residence at Block 782 Pasir Ris St 71 celebrates the timeless warmth and textural depth of mid-century modern design. At the threshold, a striking floor-to-ceiling white geometric breeze block screen wall establishes an architectural statement, filtering gentle ambient daylight into the foyer while delineating the entryway with grace. Rich walnut millwork integrates seamless shoe storage with a recessed warm-lit display niche and an upholstered bench settee, underscored by earthy terracotta quarry tiles that flow continuously into the kitchen and out toward the expansive curved balcony.

The kitchen serves as an inspiring culinary showpiece, balancing vintage character with functional precision. Dark walnut cabinetry with handleless profiles pairs with handcrafted sage green zellige backsplash tiles, illuminated by seamless under-cabinet LED strip lighting. A dedicated coffee station with a pull-out espresso tray, a tiled breakfast counter peninsula, and a statement stainless-steel Bertazzoni range cooker with classic red dials anchor the space with artisanal prestige.

In the open-concept living hall, a recessed circular ceiling cove with a sleek matte black fan crowns an inviting relaxation zone. Low-profile rattan and cane-front media cabinetry pairs with warm acoustics, Marshall soundbar, lush indoor botanicals, and a dedicated vinyl listening console complete with turntable, record storage, and illuminated glass vitrines. The private quarters extend this tranquil warmth: the master bedroom pairs dark timber flooring with an emerald green velvet headboard, nursery cot corner, curved island chest of drawers, floor-to-ceiling charcoal wardrobes, and arched full-height mirror reflecting the serene ensuite with reeded fluted glass screens. Completed in thoughtful partnership between LuxCraft and Space Wonder, this home stands as an inspiring testament to bespoke spatial craftsmanship.
